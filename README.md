# warrantied.app

Static brochure website for Warrantied Application.

## Internal product notes

This file contains internal planning notes and should not be included in the public-facing brochure content.

### Product-market fit signals

- Discovery sources: search traffic, AI-assisted discovery, social/community mentions, direct referrals, creator mentions, and repeat visits.
- Engagement signals: waitlist signups, CTA clicks by page section, scroll depth on key product messages, time on page, and return visits.
- Product interest: repeat visits, email interest, waitlist conversions, shares to friends or communities, and direct feedback on value and use cases.

### Suggested engagement tracking plan

#### Core events
- Hero CTA click
- Waitlist signup
- Pricing or early-access interest
- Feature section views
- Referral source capture

#### Source tracking
- Search and organic landing pages
- AI chat and assistant referrals
- Social posts and communities
- Direct links and partner referrals

#### Signal benchmarks
- Waitlist conversion rate
- Top-performing acquisition channels
- Most engaged headline or CTA
- Strongest customer segments

### Internal positioning notes

- Warrantied is positioned around tracking purchases, warranty coverage, service reminders, and claim timing for home and family purchases.
- The current landing page is an information-only brochure intended to test interest and refine product direction.
- There is no pricing in place yet; early access is a validation step while the product is being shaped.
- Typical use cases include appliances, electronics, household items, furniture, free service-period tracking, and warranty-extension planning.
