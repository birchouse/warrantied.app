Why it matters

Most warranties are forgotten until it is too late.

People buy appliances, electronics, furniture, and household items every month, but the real issue is not the purchase itself — it is keeping the coverage alive and remembering the right moment to act.

Warrantied brings that visibility back into everyday life: simple records, clear reminders, and a reliable place to manage the things that matter financially.

Typical use cases

  • Home appliance and furniture purchases
  • Electronics and white goods
  • Family purchases across multiple retailers
  • Free service period tracking
  • Warranty extension planning
